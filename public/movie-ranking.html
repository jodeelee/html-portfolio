The Best TV Shows of All Time According to Deepa

My top 3 TV shows of all-time.

Outlander

I enjoyed watching this series because the story line is rich, for it's interesting characters, and the female lead is empowering.

Friends

I can watch this series over and over again because it was a big part of my youth. I love all of the characters and find every episode is hilarious.

The Office

I love the characters in this series and find them absolutely ridiculous. I also find the romance between Jim and Pam endearing.
